PROJECT: Don’t Look Human
ENGINE: Godot 4.7, low-poly 3D, single-player prototype.

CORE PITCH:
You play as a suspiciously smart animal blending in with NPC animals. Walk normally near a ranger to appear natural; sprinting near the ranger raises suspicion. Steal food and escape.

CURRENT PLAYABLE LOOP:
1. Player moves with WASD.
2. Hold Shift to sprint.
3. Ranger slowly patrols.
4. Walking normally near ranger is safe.
5. Sprinting within ranger range fills suspicion bar.
6. At 100% suspicion: “CAUGHT! Press R to retry.”
7. Collect yellow/orange picnic food.
8. Reach green exit pad.
9. “YOU ESCAPED! Press R to play again.”
10. R reloads the scene after either catch or win.

CONTROLS / INPUT MAP:
- move_left = A
- move_right = D
- move_forward = W
- move_back = S
- run = Shift
- restart = R

WORKING SYSTEMS:
- player.gd:
  - CharacterBody3D movement
  - Walk speed 1.2
  - Run speed 4.0
  - Gravity and ground handling
- npc_animal.gd:
  - NPC animals randomly walk, pause, change direction, and stay inside a radius.
- ranger.gd:
  - Ranger patrols randomly at speed 0.8 inside radius 7.
  - Detection range = 4.
  - Sprint speed threshold = 2.
  - Suspicion gain = 35/sec; decay = 15/sec.
  - Updates RangerStatus and SuspicionBar.
- picnic_food.gd:
  - Food disappears when player is within 1.2 units.
  - Updates ObjectiveStatus.
- exit_area.gd:
  - Uses Area3D body_entered signal.
  - Refuses exit until PicnicFood is gone.
  - Wins when Player enters after food is collected.
  - R restarts after escaping.
